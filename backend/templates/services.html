FAQ

Questions we hear often

Who owns my website once it is done?

You do. Once the project is complete and paid for, the domain, content and website belong to your business.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Every website comes with an easy admin panel to change text, images and products, and we train you to use it.

Do you provide the domain and hosting?

Yes. We help you choose a domain, set up secure hosting with SSL, and can handle renewals under our maintenance plan.

Can my online store accept payments?

Yes. We integrate Razorpay so your customers can pay by UPI, cards, net banking and wallets.

Will you publish my app on the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We build apps for both Android and iOS and publish them on both stores for you.

Is marketing a one-time job or monthly?

Both are available. Choose a one-time setup, or a monthly plan where we keep improving your visibility.

What does the maintenance plan (AMC) cover?

Updates, backups, security checks, content changes and hosting renewal, so you never have to worry.

How much will my project cost?

Every business is different, so we give you a free, clear quote after understanding your needs. No hidden costs.
